Add to Home Screen — On iPhone tap Share → "Add to Home Screen". Always open from there to keep your data saved.
📋
Back up regularly — Tap 📋 to copy your data or ✉ to email it to yourself. Do this after a big trading or collecting session so you never lose your progress.
↩
Restore anytime — Lost your progress? Tap ↩ and paste your saved backup to get everything back instantly.
📖
Using the Album tab
• Tap once → sticker turns green ✓ — you have it
• Tap again → turns blue 🤝 — a friend has it for you
• Tap once more → back to white — uncollected
• Press & hold a white sticker to open the trade screen — perfect for in-person trading. When someone offers you a sticker you need, hold it, search and select which of your duplicates you're giving them in exchange, and confirm. The sticker marks itself as collected and your stock updates instantly — no manual steps needed.
🤝
Using the Trades tab
• Add friends by pasting their 📋 backup JSON
• The app automatically shows what they have for you 🟢 and what you have for them 🔵
• Update a friend's data anytime with ✏️ when they collect new stickers
• Works over WhatsApp, iMessage, email — no live connection needed
📦
Inventory tab — Track your duplicate stickers here. Use + / − to manage quantities. The Trades tab uses this to show friends what you can offer them.
📦
What is the Inventory tab? — This is where you track your duplicate stickers — the ones you have more than one copy of and are willing to trade away.
➕
Adding duplicates — Use the + button on any sticker to add a copy to your inventory. Use − to remove one when you've traded it away.
🔵
Blue badge — The blue number on each sticker shows how many copies you have available to trade.
🤝
Used in trades — The Trades tab uses your inventory to show friends what you can offer them. Keep it updated for accurate trade matches!
👆
Quick trade — In the Album tab, press & hold a missing sticker to open the trade screen. It pulls directly from your inventory so you can pick what to give away on the spot.
⚠️ Keep it fresh: Ask friends to send you a new backup after they collect new stickers — the comparison is only as accurate as the last backup they shared. They can use 📋 to copy or ✉ to email it directly to you.
This is not a live platform — there's no account or chat. Think of it as a smart comparison tool: you each keep your own copy of the app, share a snapshot of your data, and the app figures out who has what for whom.
  1. Share the app link with your friend so they can track their own album
  2. When ready to compare, ask them to tap 📋 to copy or ✉ to email you their backup. It works both ways — share your own backup with them too so they can add you on their end.
  3. Tap + Add Friend below, enter their name and paste their text
  4. The app instantly shows what they can give you 🟢 and what you can give them 🔵
  5. Agree on the swap and update your albums accordingly
  6. Tap 📱 on a friend's card anytime to send them a text reminder to share their latest backup — keeps the comparison fresh as you both collect more stickers
